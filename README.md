# Dr. Mohammadmahdi Ariannejad
### Assistant Professor · Electrical & Electronics Engineering
**Xiamen University Malaysia** · Malaysia

**Research interests:** Robotics · Embedded Intelligence · Power Electronics · Smart Energy Systems · Semiconductor Technologies

I am an assistant professor and engineering researcher working across electronic systems, robotics, and intelligent technologies. My interests connect fundamental engineering principles with practical implementation—from circuit-level design and microcontrollers to autonomous systems and energy applications.

My approach combines **analytical modelling, simulation, hardware experimentation, and reproducible engineering education**.
